# Conversion (approved 24 Nov 2026)

How Half Byte sites lead a local-service visitor to call, text, use a tool, or ask for a quote — without
pushing. This is also what "caring" looks like. Do not copy text, photos, or components from the references.

Full write-up and sources: the Page "The science of leading visitors to act." Proposals P1–P22 below are
**adopted**. Tools T4–T6 had no response from Jake and stay as references only, not defaults.

## Decision rules Jake named

1. **Value before contact (the anti-paywall rule).** Jake rarely finishes tools that demand all his information
   before showing value, because it feels like a paywall. Every pre-contact tool **shows value and a price
   range before asking for contact details**. Contact info comes **last** and is **optional for seeing the
   result**. Ask for the fewest fields. "Send me this estimate" or "Book a walk-through" is when we ask.
2. **Build a quote or estimator tool only when the client can supply real pricing inputs.** Otherwise use a
   qualifying multi-step form (Adair / Schumacher style). Never ship placeholder numbers. A tool that scores
   "no data" is a labeled placeholder plus the list of facts Jake must get from the client.
3. **Exact quotes only if the client can quantify** (Eco Home). If they can supply rates, show ranges (or
   exact figures they stand behind). If they can't, don't fake a calculator.

## Adopted rules (P1–P22)

### CTA and trust (P1–P5)

- **One primary action per page**, written into `plan.md`. Only the primary uses the filled button style. Call and text are quiet secondaries (`tel:` and, if they answer texts, `sms:`). Copy is verb + outcome ("Start your project", "Get my build estimate"), never "Submit" or "Learn more" as the primary.
- **Microcopy** under the primary button: effort, time, and what happens next ("Takes about two minutes. We reply the same business day.").
- Place the primary CTA in the header (desktop), the hero, after work or proof, after reviews, the closing band, and the footer. Same wording and destination everywhere.
- **Mobile action bar:** 2–3 actions (Call, Text, Quote), at least 48px plus `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`, hidden on the quote page and while the keyboard is open, minimal motion. Hiller is the reference.
- **Proof next to the ask:** a rating, review count, license, or guarantee microline in the same viewport as every primary CTA and form. Real figures, linked to the Google profile when we have one.
- **Plain language:** body copy at about grade 5–7. Headings start with the information-bearing words. Ban "Why Choose Us", "peace of mind", "quality craftsmanship", and other lines any firm could use.
- **Banned patterns:** pop-ups or interstitials on load; self-opening chat; fake urgency, fake counters, or "Jen just booked"; pre-checked consent; confirmshaming; **gating a price or result behind a phone number**; fake or AI-written reviews.

### Planning (P6–P10)

- Every page in `plan.md` gets a stage, one primary action, a secondary, and its trust elements.
- **Tool evaluation** is a required site-plan step (see below).
- Intake collects the client's top objections; the plan assigns each to a section or the FAQ.
- **City or service-area pages** only where there is real local proof (a project, a review, or a crew who works there). Local H1, local photos, local review, local specifics, CTA prefilled with the town. No doorway pages.
- A **measurement plan** ships with every site (`brief/measurement.md`, from `templates/measurement-plan.md`).

### Forms, tools, phone, thank-you (P11–P15)

- Default quote form: 3–5 visible fields (name, phone or email with one required, service, optional details).
- **Multi-step qualifier** (Adair / Schumacher / Bath Fitter "start your design"): easy questions first (town, land, timeline, budget range), **contact last**. Show progress. Carry answers into hidden fields. If a picker or CTA links to the qualifier with query params (`?work=`, `?path=`, `?land=`, `?timeline=`, `?project=`, `?chapter=`), the form **must read those params on load** and set the corresponding fields (including hidden ones). A `?path=`, `?work=`, `?project=`, or `?chapter=` that is not applied is a fail.
- **Build-brief wizard (C33):** when the site ships a build brief, it lives on `/plan/`. A "Build your path" (or equivalent) picker's primary "Start your home" goes to `/plan/` with `?work=`, `?path=`, `?land=`, and `?timeline=` as they apply. `/plan/` must read those params on load (and any other brief fields in the query) and set the corresponding fields. The short `/contact/` qualifier still honors the same params for people who skip the wizard or use "Rather just talk?" (that exit carries answers so far). No dollar figures in the wizard. Send the brief through the existing contact / quote endpoint; the office email is the formatted brief.
- Visible labels, correct `type` and `autocomplete`, inline validation on blur, Cloudflare Turnstile (no puzzle CAPTCHAs). Hidden fields for service, tool inputs, page, and UTMs. If the visitor saved houses (C28d), the qualifier or quote form includes a "Houses you liked" field (hidden or visible, `data-saved-houses-field`) prefilled from `localStorage`. The Start-your-home CTA reads "Start your home (N saved)" when N > 0. No fake counts.
- **Tool contract:** rates from `src/data/estimator.ts`. `null` hides the result and shows the CTA. Show an honest **range**, what's included and excluded, the pricing date, and "final price after a site visit." One "Send me this estimate" CTA that carries inputs. `aria-live` on the result, keyboard support, no slider-only inputs, hydrate when visible. **The range is visible without contact details.**
- Every phone number is a `tel:` link, visible in the header on desktop and in the action bar on mobile. Hours beside the number when we have them. `sms:` only if they answer.
- **Thank-you page** per form: confirm what they sent, who will reply and by when (a face if we have one), one optional next step, fire the conversion event, `noindex`. No upsell pop-ups.
- Financing copy is Reg Z–safe: link to the lender's prequal; do not compute monthly payments without full disclosures.

### Review and measurement (P16–P22)

- Conversion audit at 1440 and 390: does screen one state the outcome, the place, the next step, and the proof? Is the primary CTA visible? One primary style? Trust beside every ask? Form field count? Targets ≥ 24px (48px for primary)?
- Speed budget on mobile still in force; hold LCP ≤ 2.5s, INP ≤ 200ms, CLS ≤ 0.1.
- Tracking test: fire every event in the preview and confirm it in the dashboard before launch.
- Every site ships the event spec, Plausible (default) or Umami, Cloudflare Web Analytics for Core Web Vitals, and GA4 + Consent Mode via Zaraz only when the client runs Google Ads. See `templates/measurement-plan.md`.
- Monthly one-page client report: `templates/monthly-client-report.md`. Promise a measured **90-day baseline** and improvement against it, not an industry-wide conversion number.

## Tool evaluation (site-plan step)

Score each candidate 1 / 3 / 5 on: value to the visitor, data readiness, build effort (inverted), and hand-off strength.

- Pick one or two highest-scoring tools.
- Any tool that scores 1 on data readiness is **shelved** until the client supplies the facts. Show a placeholder and list what Jake must get. Never invent rates.
- **Default for a custom-home builder without pricing data:** do not ship an estimator. Ship a C33 build-brief wizard on `/plan/` when the brief (sketch, look-at-first, similar houses) is the point of the intake; otherwise an Adair/Schumacher-style multi-step "Start your home" qualifier, contact last. The short qualifier remains on `/contact/` even when C33 is present.
- **Default when they have cost-per-sqft (or equivalent) ranges:** a build-budget planner (size × finish tier → range; land and site costs listed separately) following the anti-paywall rule. Eco Home / billyGO are the references.

Industry starting points (only when data exists): custom home → budget planner or lot-fit checker; land clearing → the 316-style estimator; roofing / HVAC / plumbing / landscaping → published ranges or a short quiz. See the conversion Page for the full matrix.

## Anti-patterns (never)

Pop-ups on load, mobile interstitials, fake countdowns, invented "X people near you" counters, pre-checked consent, confirmshaming, gated prices, fake reviews, chat that opens itself over the hero. Mr. Rooter is the counter-example: an offer banner, an "Act Now" popup, and a call bar all competing before the visitor has read a word. Keep a call bar; drop the stack of overlays.
